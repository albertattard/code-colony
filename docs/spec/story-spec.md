# Story Spec

## Purpose

This document defines the narrative frame for Code Colony.

It is the canonical source for the game's setting, player role, corporate framing, known facts, hidden truths, and narrative constraints. Mission specs should use this document as the source of truth for story continuity and only add the mission-specific story details relevant to that slice.

This is not a full lore bible. It should stay concise, practical, and usable during development.

## Scope

This spec defines:

- the player's place in the world
- the setting and initial situation
- the public framing presented to the player
- the narrative role of Helix Dynamics
- the intended tone
- the distinction between known information and hidden truth
- how the mystery should unfold over time

This spec does not define:

- the exact content of every mission briefing
- final scripts for every in-game message
- implementation details for storing content in the application

## Narrative Premise

Code Colony takes place in a future where off-world colonies are established and maintained by powerful corporate operators.

The player is an engineer employed by Helix Dynamics, a company known publicly as a leader in sustainable off-world development. From an orbital command station around the planet Eryndor-IV, the player is assigned to oversee the recovery of a remote colony site after an unexpected systems outage.

Communication with the colony surface is limited. Direct deployment has been suspended. The player must instead operate robotic units remotely while restoring enough infrastructure to understand what happened below.

## Player Role

The player is:

- an engineer employed by Helix Dynamics
- stationed in orbit rather than on the colony surface
- responsible for remote recovery and operational assessment
- expected to restore systems and verify that the colony can return to standard function

The player should feel competent, methodical, and increasingly suspicious as more systems come back online.

## CORE Units

`CORE` stands for `Colony Operations and Repair Engineer`.

CORE units are specialized robotic systems used to:

- maintain infrastructure
- execute precise operational directives
- inspect systems in hazardous or inaccessible environments
- keep colony functions running when human presence is limited or unsafe

The player interacts with the colony primarily through these machines.

## Helix Dynamics

Helix Dynamics should be presented initially as:

- professional
- safety-focused
- disciplined
- confident
- committed to continuity and transparency

Its official voice should sound calm, corporate, and controlled.

The company should provide the player with enough authority to act, while also shaping the information the player receives.

## Opening Situation

At the start of the game, the player knows:

- a colony site on Eryndor-IV has suffered a serious systems outage
- the situation is officially described as contained
- communication with the surface is degraded
- direct personnel deployment has been suspended
- partial network access has been restored
- CORE units can be used to begin recovery

This is enough context to begin play without overwhelming the learner with lore.

## Known Facts Versus Hidden Truth

The story should distinguish clearly between:

- what the player is told
- what the player can confirm through restored systems
- what remains hidden

### 1. Known To The Player

At the beginning, the player is told only the operational framing and the recovery objective.

The player should not yet know:

- the true cause of the outage
- the full condition of the colony
- whether Helix Dynamics is withholding information
- whether the failure was accidental, systemic, or deliberate

### 2. Confirmable Through Play

As missions progress, the player should be able to confirm:

- the state of colony infrastructure
- inconsistencies between official messaging and on-site evidence
- signs of interrupted work, missing personnel, or abnormal events
- that the outage is more complex than a routine systems failure

### 3. Hidden Truth

The deeper truth behind the colony failure should remain unrevealed early on.

For now, this truth may remain only partially specified, but future missions should be written in a way that preserves:

- mystery
- escalation
- ambiguity in corporate intent
- room for a later coherent reveal

## Tone

The narrative tone should be:

- mysterious
- technical
- controlled
- quietly uneasy
- optimistic on the surface

The story should avoid horror as the primary mode. Unease should come from absence, inconsistency, and discovery rather than shock.

## Story Delivery Rules

Lore should be delivered gradually through:

- mission briefings
- system messages
- status updates
- recovered logs or fragments
- visible environmental clues

The story should not rely on long exposition blocks.

Each reveal should support the player's motivation to continue restoring the colony.

## Relationship To Mission Specs

Mission specs should not attempt to redefine the broader narrative frame.

Instead, each mission spec should document:

- what the player is told in that mission
- what the mission reveals or hints at
- how the mission advances the mystery or trust questions

This keeps global lore in one place and mission-level story beats in another.

## Working Story Constraints

Until later revisions say otherwise:

- Helix Dynamics is not openly villainous at the start
- the player's initial trust in the company should feel reasonable
- the early missions should focus on restoration before major narrative doubt
- story reveals should not distract from the teaching goals of beginner missions
- the mystery should expand in step with the learner's programming abilities

## Open Questions

- What caused the outage on Eryndor-IV
- Whether Helix Dynamics is hiding negligence, a failed experiment, or a broader political truth
- Whether survivors, recordings, or autonomous systems remain active on the surface
- How quickly the player should begin doubting the official corporate account
- Whether later colonies or stations should broaden the story beyond a single site
