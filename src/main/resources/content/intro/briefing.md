# Mission Briefing

## Summary
Helix Dynamics has assigned you to oversee the recovery of a remote colony site from orbit around Eryndor-IV.

## Objective
Investigate the colony site and restore critical systems in stages.

## Briefing Title
Operational Briefing

## Briefing
You are an engineer employed by Helix Dynamics, a leader in sustainable off-world development and the driving force behind humanity's expansion beyond Earth. From your command station in orbit around the planet Eryndor-IV, you have been assigned to oversee the recovery of one of the company's remote colony sites following an unexpected systems outage.

While the situation has been classified as contained, communication with the surface remains limited. As a precaution, all direct deployment has been suspended.

Instead, you will operate CORE units (*Colony Operations and Repair Engineers*), specialized robotic systems designed to maintain infrastructure, execute complex directives, and ensure continuity in challenging environments. With partial access to the colony's network restored, your role is to re-establish critical systems, assess operational integrity, and return the site to standard function.

Helix Dynamics is committed to transparency, safety, and the continued success of its colonies.

Your work will ensure that commitment is upheld.
